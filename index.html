WHY SMALL AI?

Small, local, transparent support

What it does

  • Runs a lightweight Naive Bayes model and explicit phrase checks in the browser.
  • Structures reported words and suggests fields the worker can confirm.
  • Shows the matched concern, potential warning phrases, and model uncertainty.
  • Works without sending case text to a cloud AI service.

Why keep it small?

  • Local processing reduces unnecessary cloud transmission and can respond quickly on constrained devices.
  • Rules and class examples can be inspected and discussed with workers.
  • Separate safety rules provide a visible guardrail, not clinical certainty.
  • A clinician or health worker—not this model—uses local guidance and decides next steps.

The model score is uncalibrated and must not be read as a probability that a person has a condition. Phrase checks can miss concerns or match incorrectly.

SAFETY · PRIVACY · LIMITATIONS

Responsible AI and shared-device privacy

Clinical safety

  • No diagnosis, prescription, or autonomous referral.
  • Potential warning signs are based on reported words and need human assessment.
  • No warning phrase detected does not mean a person is safe.
  • Human health worker remains the final decision-maker.
  • Bangla/Banglish synthetic examples are not representative clinical evidence.

Where data sits / who can read it

  • Saved case records sit in this browser’s localStorage on this device.
  • Anyone with access to the device/browser profile may be able to read the records.
  • LocalStorage is not encrypted by this prototype. Shared or lost phones create privacy risk.
  • Clear records when appropriate; production use needs authentication, encryption, and access controls.
  • No names, NID, PIN, OTP, bank password, or real payment details are requested.

Other limits

  • Prototype classifier and synthetic evaluation are not clinically validated.
  • Sample facilities may be incomplete; no live capacity or distance calculation.
  • Micro-protection, support, payment plans, contributions, SMS, and sync are illustrative or simulated only.
  • No real NGO, insurer, mobile-money, transport, or care partnership is claimed.
  • Deployment requires Bangladesh clinical, regulatory, privacy, security, and implementation review.
