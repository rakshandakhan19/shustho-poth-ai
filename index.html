Design rationale

Why Small AI?

What a fixed form does

A form can store structured information. SMS can transmit fixed fields. But frontline conversations do not always arrive in structured fields.

What this prototype adds

A narrow local classifier converts messy Bangla/Banglish descriptions into structured information and flags uncertainty for human review.

Design principle: AI handles a narrow information-structuring task. It does not replace the health worker.
Safety & governance

Responsible AI

  • No autonomous diagnosis or treatment.
  • Missing information and low confidence are surfaced.
  • The human health worker makes the final decision.
  • DGHS facility records are a small registry sample, not live availability.
  • Financial/mobile-money options are access workflow only; no real payment is initiated.
  • Synthetic cases are clearly labeled and are not clinical records.
  • Model errors are retained and exposed rather than hidden.
