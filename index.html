Sample records use fields from the public DGHS Facility Registry. This demo does not calculate distance or claim live service availability.

Source: DGHS Facility Registry. Verify live facility details before deployment.

Why AI?

A fixed form can store fields and SMS can transmit them. This narrow offline model adds value by classifying and structuring unstructured local-language descriptions, including Bangla/Banglish, while exposing uncertainty for human review.

Responsible AI

  • No autonomous diagnosis or treatment.
  • Missing information and low confidence are surfaced.
  • Human health worker makes the final call.
  • DGHS facility records are a small registry sample, not live availability.
  • Financial/mobile-money options are access workflow only; no real payment is initiated.
  • Synthetic cases are not clinical records.
