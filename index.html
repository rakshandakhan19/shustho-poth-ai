WHY SMALL AI?

Small, local, transparent support

What it does

  • Runs a lightweight Naive Bayes model and separate explicit phrase-based safety checks in the browser.
  • Structures natural Bangla/Banglish words into presentation/access categories and fields.
  • Shows uncertainty; no model result is a diagnosis or treatment recommendation.
  • Works without sending case text to a cloud AI service.

Why Small AI instead of SMS, a spreadsheet, or search?

  • SMS can transmit a message. A spreadsheet can store a case. Search can retrieve information. The small local model interprets short Bangla/Banglish descriptions into a limited set of concern and access labels.
  • It does not diagnose or decide care; explicit rules surface possible warning words and a person decides the next step.
  • It runs in the browser without sending case text to a cloud AI service. SMS is only a prepared message in this demo; nothing is sent.

The raw model score is not shown to patients. Phrase checks can miss concerns or match incorrectly. Human professionals make clinical decisions.

BANGLADESH LOCALIZATION

Why these data?

  • Local language phrases are synthetic prototype examples for the classifier; they are not patient records.
  • Bangladesh National Portal geography powers optional division → district → upazila selection; it is not proof that care exists nearby.
  • DGHS Facility Registry contributes 20,509 selected active public records across 8 divisions and 64 named districts for care navigation. It does not report live services or capacity; Bangla-to-English locality matching remains limited.
  • WHO Bangladesh indicators are population context only, never classifier or individual-level evidence.
  • Service Delivery Indicators, Global Findex and GSMA have no verified Bangladesh values bundled in this version.

These indicators inform the prototype's priority areas. They are contextual health evidence, not diagnostic evidence.

SAFETY · PRIVACY · LIMITATIONS

Responsible AI and shared-device privacy

Safety

  • No diagnosis, prescription, or autonomous referral.
  • Potential warning signs are based on reported words and require urgent human assessment where appropriate.
  • No warning phrase detected does not mean a person is safe.
  • Health professionals remain the final decision-makers.
  • Examples are synthetic and do not represent clinical evidence or all Bangladeshi dialects.

Where data sits / who can read it

  • Health records and case data are stored in this browser’s localStorage on this device.
  • Anyone with access to the device/browser profile may potentially read them.
  • LocalStorage is not encrypted. Shared or lost phones create privacy risk.
  • Use Hide record or Clear local demo data when appropriate.
  • Production needs encryption, authentication, role-based access, consent records, audit logs, revocation, and secure deletion.

Other limits

  • No clinical validation, hospital integration, live NGO integration, or live emergency dispatch.
  • Facility sample data are incomplete and not live; verify services and location locally.
  • Financial transaction, community request, access code, SMS, and sync are simulations, not real services.
  • No real NGO, insurer, mobile-money, transport, or care partnership is claimed.
  • Deployment needs local clinical, regulatory, privacy, security, and implementation review.
